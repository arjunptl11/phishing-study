Important Information

The purpose of this study is to evaluate how people interact with and respond to different website interface designs. Your child may participate if they are between the ages of 13 and 17 and a regular internet user. They will be shown a series of website screenshots and asked to rate their responses to each one. The survey takes approximately 10 minutes. There is no compensation for participating. Risks are minimal, similar to those in a typical school survey. Your child will not benefit directly, but the findings may contribute to improvements in how digital interfaces are designed.

Why are we doing this study?

We are conducting this study to better understand how people evaluate and respond to different designs of website interfaces. Understanding how young internet users respond to different interface designs is a valuable part of this research.

Why is your child being asked to participate?

Your child is being invited to participate because they are between the ages of 13 and 17 and a regular internet user. They were contacted through the personal or professional networks of the research team. Your permission and your child's assent are both required before they may participate.

What will your child be asked to do?

If you give permission and your child agrees, they will complete an online survey on Formbricks (app.formbricks.com). The survey includes:

  • An age verification step.
  • This parent permission form, which must be confirmed before your child continues.
  • A minor assent form your child will read and agree to before continuing.
  • Twelve website screenshots. For each screenshot, your child will rate their reactions on a 5-point scale and indicate how confident they are.
  • A few exit questions about the screens they saw.
  • Demographic questions about age range, technology use, and internet experience.
  • A debriefing statement with additional information about the study.

The survey takes approximately 10 minutes. Your child may stop at any time.

Note About Study Procedures

To ensure the accuracy of responses, some additional details about the study's focus will be shared with participants at the end of the survey in a debriefing statement. This is a standard and IRB-approved research practice.

What are the risks?

The risks are minimal and consistent with typical online surveys. Your child may feel mild frustration with some of the questions. The likelihood of any harm occurring is considered very unlikely.

Are there benefits?

There are no direct benefits to your child from participating. The information gathered may contribute to improvements in how digital interfaces are designed.

How will privacy be protected?

Your child's responses are anonymous. No names, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information are collected. If your child optionally provides an email address for a follow-up conversation, that address will be stored separately from all survey responses and deleted once follow-up contact is complete. Response data is stored in a secure Google Cloud repository accessible only to the research team.

Is participation voluntary?

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may refuse permission, and your child may decline or stop at any time, without penalty. If your child chooses to stop, they simply close the browser window.

Questions?

For questions about this study, contact Arjun Patel at patel27a4@ncssm.edu or Dr. Cori Faklaris at cfaklari@charlotte.edu. For questions about your child's rights as a research participant, contact the UNC Charlotte Office of Research Protections and Integrity at uncc-irb@charlotte.edu.

Concise Summary

This will take approximately 10 minutes. There is no compensation for participating. Risks are minimal. Your responses are anonymous. If you are interested in learning more, please continue reading below.

Why are we doing this study?

We are conducting this study to better understand how people evaluate and respond to different designs of website interfaces. The findings will contribute to research on user experience and interface design.

Why are you being asked to participate?

You are being invited to participate because you are 18 years of age or older and a regular internet user. You were contacted through the personal or professional networks of the research team.

What will you be asked to do?

If you agree to participate, you will complete an online survey hosted on Formbricks (app.formbricks.com). The survey proceeds as follows:

  • You will read this consent form and click to agree to participate.
  • You will be shown 12 website screenshots. For each screenshot, you will rate your reactions on a 5-point scale and indicate how confident you are in your response.
  • You will answer a few questions about the screens you saw.
  • You will answer demographic questions about your age range, technology use, and internet experience.
  • You will receive a debriefing statement with additional information about the study.

The entire survey takes approximately 10 minutes to complete.

Note About Study Procedures

To ensure the accuracy of your responses, some additional details about the study's focus will be shared with you at the end of the survey in a debriefing statement. This is a standard and IRB-approved research practice.

What are the risks and discomforts?

The risks associated with participating in this study are minimal and consistent with those encountered in other online surveys. You may feel mild discomfort or frustration with some of the questions. The likelihood of any harm occurring is considered very unlikely.

Are there any benefits to participating?

There are no direct benefits to you from participating in this study. However, the information we learn may contribute to improvements in how websites and digital interfaces are designed.

How will your privacy be protected?

Your responses are anonymous. We do not collect your name, phone number, or any other directly identifying information as part of the survey. The only optional identifier collected is an email address, if you voluntarily indicate willingness to be contacted for a follow-up conversation. If provided, this email address will be stored in a separate file with no connection to your survey responses and will be deleted once follow-up contact is complete. Survey response data will be stored in a secure Google Cloud repository accessible only to the research team.

Is participation voluntary?

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or to stop at any time without penalty. If you choose to withdraw, you may simply close the browser window.

What if you have questions?

If you have questions about this study, please contact the Student Investigator, Arjun Patel, at patel27a4@ncssm.edu, or the Principal Investigator, Dr. Cori Faklaris, at cfaklari@charlotte.edu.

If you have questions about your rights as a research participant or concerns about how this study is being conducted, please contact the UNC Charlotte Office of Research Protections and Integrity (ORPI) at uncc-irb@charlotte.edu.
